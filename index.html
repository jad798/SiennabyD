Organizing process

Walkthrough. Edit. Arrange.

How an organizing visit works. Each step makes the next one easier to decide, so the result lasts.

Walkthrough

We see the space with you and agree what stays, what goes and how you want to live in the room.

Edit

Everything is sorted by category. Duplicates, damaged items and things you no longer use are set aside to donate or discard.

Arrange

What stays goes into matching containers, folded, labelled and styled, so the finished room looks designed.
